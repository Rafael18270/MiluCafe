About

A living room in the city

Milu Café was opened in 2016 by Dan and Smaranda Milu. Press coverage describes it as a warm, hygge-like space rather than an industrial-style coffee bar: shelves of books and curios, paintings on the walls and a cosy, lived-in feel.

The café has since moved to Strada Nicolae Filipescu 52, beside Gherea Park, and has appeared in many Bucharest best-coffee lists. It also welcomes artists and art lovers, with exhibition opportunities, and a street-art mural marks the corner.

It is a place for a quiet break, a good book or a meeting with friends.
